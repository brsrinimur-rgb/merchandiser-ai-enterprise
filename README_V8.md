# Merchandiser AI Enterprise v8

Interactive decision-experience upgrade built on the verified v7 backend.

## New in v8
- Clickable executive KPI drill-downs for inventory risk, revenue risk, recovery opportunities, and OTB.
- Persistent AI summary dock available from every screen.
- Enhanced Retail Digital Twin with quantity, promotion, lead-time, safety-stock, and demand-uplift controls.
- Product 360 action bar linking directly to Digital Twin, Explainability, and Approval Board.
- Improved workflow-board interactions and decision navigation.
- Existing v7 APIs and modules preserved.

Run `run_all.bat`, then open http://127.0.0.1:5500.
